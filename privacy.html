Privacy Policy

Jocelyn develops DON'T THINK NORMAL, also titled 别按常理出牌！. This policy covers the current offline, free-hint version of the game. Privacy contact: seansheaton@gmail.com. Effective date: September 9, 2026.

Game data stays on your device

The game does not send personal data or gameplay data to us or to third-party services. It has no account, advertising SDK, in-app purchases, online analytics SDK, or cloud save. We do not sell or share your in-game data.

Progress and settings

Completed and skipped levels, your current level, unlocked levels, hints viewed, language, music, sound, and vibration preferences are saved in the app's local storage so you can continue playing. A local backup helps recover an interrupted save. These files remain on your device; Android cloud backup is disabled for the app.

Motion and permissions

Some puzzles use acceleration and gravity readings to detect shaking, tilting, or turning the phone upside down. These readings are processed temporarily on the device and are not stored as a motion history or transmitted. The vibration permission provides optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo library.

Local diagnostics

The game temporarily processes level interactions in memory. Test builds may also write local diagnostic logs, such as level actions and timing, to help troubleshoot the game. These records are not automatically uploaded; the game contains no remote crash-reporting service.

Retention, deletion, and security

Local progress and settings remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. On Android, open system Settings, select the app, then Storage and Clear data (wording varies by device). This also removes the local save backup and cannot be undone. There is no server account or cloud copy for us to delete or restore. Android's app storage protections limit access to the local files; keep your device secure.

Contacting support

If you choose to email Jocelyn, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you send. We use these only to respond to your request, resolve issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. Email is handled by our email provider, Gmail. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary personal information. Support messages are retained only as long as needed for those purposes. You can contact seansheaton@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent us, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Children's privacy

The game's local-only data handling applies to all players. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the game. If a child has sent personal information through support email, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

This website and external services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies, or forms. GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses and request logs to provide and secure hosting, under its own privacy statement. Opening an email link or using an app store involves those providers and their own privacy policies. These website and support activities are separate from the offline game's operation.

Policy updates

If a future version changes data handling, such as adding an online service or advertising, we will update this policy and provide any required notice or consent before that processing begins. The effective date above identifies this policy version.
